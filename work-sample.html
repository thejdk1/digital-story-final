Más que solo ‘Chinito'

Andrew Xian Fernandez is an Asian-American born in Anyang, China in September of 2010. After a heart condition threatened his life at birth, he had emergency surgery that his biological parents were not able to maintain. He was placed in the Chinese adoption system for three years, and adopted by a Spanish/Salvadoran family in 2014. Since then, Andrew has battled the ideas of identity and belonging, having to deal with frequent questions about his last name in comparison to his physical appearance. Andrew has expressed not only a desire to reconnect with his Chinese ancestry, but also one to learn more about his place in Latino culture. With the help of proactive members of the UNC Chapel Hill Latinx community, Andrew was presented the idea of a photowalk where he would be able engage with, learn from, and simply talk to potential mentors and friends in his journey.

By Julian Fernandez · September 7, 2026
